🏛️ Direct Scholarly & Commentary Vault — Jeremiah 1:5 (`yada` & `qadash`)
Scripture Central / Donald W. Parry: The Hebrew verb yādaʿ (“I knew thee”) denotes intimate covenant relationship and premortal acquaintance—not mere foresight. Paired with hikdîšeka (“I sanctified/consecrated thee”), Jeremiah 1:5 is one of the clearest Old Testament witnesses of premortal identity and foreordination (compare Abraham 3:22–23; Alma 13:3).
