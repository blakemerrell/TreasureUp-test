Step 2 of 4 · The Crucible (Separating Bedrock Values from Good Habits)

“If you have more than 3 core values, you have a wish list.”

Most people pull 6–8 good values in Round 1. To discover which 3 are your true bedrock (and which ones are supporting sub-values underneath them), answer these two mirror questions first:

🏔️ Mirror 1: The Peak Alive Moment

Think of a day in the last year when you went to bed exhausted, proud, and deeply alive. What were you doing, and what value were you honoring?

⚡ Mirror 2: The Frustration Shadow (ACT Method)

What triggers or drains you fastest when it’s violated? (Your sharpest frustration is almost always the flip-side of your highest core value.)

Now Crown Your Top 3 Bedrock Cards

From the cards you pulled in Step 1, tap your Top 3 Bedrock Pillars. The remaining cards in your pile won’t be lost—they automatically become your Supporting Sub-Values!
